# 4. Shipping and delivery  [audience: customer]

| **Zone** | **States (examples)** | **Delivery time** | **Fee** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lagos | All Lagos LGAs | 1–2 working days | ₦2,500; FREE on orders of ₦50,000 or more |
| South-West | Ogun, Oyo, Osun, Ondo, Ekiti | 2–4 working days | ₦4,000 |
| Other states + FCT | Abuja, Rivers, Kano, Enugu, etc. | 3–6 working days | ₦5,500 |
| Pickup (Ikeja) | — | Ready same day if ordered before 12:00 | Free |

Orders placed before 14:00 on a working day are dispatched the same day; later orders the next working day.

Customers receive a tracking link by SMS and email when the order is dispatched.

Delivery is attempted twice. After two failed attempts the parcel returns to the warehouse; redelivery costs the zone fee again, or the customer can collect it at the pickup point free of charge.

Orders count as delayed if not delivered 2 working days after the latest estimate; delayed customers can ask for the delivery fee to be refunded.

Addresses can be changed only before dispatch (see section 8).
