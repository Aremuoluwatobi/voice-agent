# 9. Warranty  [audience: customer]

Warranty periods are listed per product in section 3 (3 months to 12 months), starting on the delivery date.

Covered: manufacturing faults (won't charge, won't power on, one side silent, battery swelling).

Not covered: water damage beyond the stated rating, drops and cracks, lost earbuds or cases, cuts in cables.

Claim: describe the fault and send a short video or photo; if confirmed, Shopdarret repairs or replaces the item within 7 working days of receiving it. If the same model is unavailable, an equal-value item or store credit is offered.

Lost single earbud: not covered; a replacement single bud can be bought for ₦12,000 (SD-EB-200) or ₦20,000 (SD-EB-350).
