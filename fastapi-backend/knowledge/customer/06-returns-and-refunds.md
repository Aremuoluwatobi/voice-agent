# 6. Returns and refunds  [audience: customer]

Change of mind: return within 7 days of delivery, unused, in original sealed packaging with all accessories.

Not returnable for change of mind: opened earbuds and in-ear products (hygiene), and opened cables and cases. Faulty or damaged items are always covered by sections 7 and 9.

How to return: request a return through the assistant or email; drop the item at the Ikeja pickup point or book a courier pickup (Lagos ₦2,500, other zones the zone fee). Return shipping is free if the item is faulty or wrong.

Refund timing after the returned item is checked (within 2 working days of arrival): card 3–5 working days; bank transfer 1–2 working days; pay on delivery: refunded by bank transfer within 2 working days.

Delivery fees are refunded only if the item was faulty, damaged or wrong.

Refunds are approved by a human team member; the AI assistant can start a return request but cannot approve a refund.
