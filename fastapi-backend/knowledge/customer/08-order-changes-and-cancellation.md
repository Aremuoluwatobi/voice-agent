# 8. Order changes and cancellation  [audience: customer]

Before dispatch: the customer can cancel, change the delivery address, or change colour/quantity. Identity check required.

After dispatch: cannot cancel or change the address; the customer can refuse delivery (pay-on-delivery) or return the item under section 6.

Cancelled prepaid orders are refunded in full using the timings in section 6.
