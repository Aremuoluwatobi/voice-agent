# 1. Business scenario

# 2. Company profile and store information  [audience: customer]

Shopdarret sells earbuds, headphones, speakers, power banks, chargers, cables, phone cases, smartwatches and creator accessories. All products are new and come with a Shopdarret warranty (section 9).

We deliver to all 36 states and the FCT. Lagos orders usually arrive in 1–2 working days.

Customers can reach us through the voice assistant on the website, by email at support@shopdarret.example, or at the Ikeja pickup point. We do not take orders by phone.

Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding Nigerian public holidays.
