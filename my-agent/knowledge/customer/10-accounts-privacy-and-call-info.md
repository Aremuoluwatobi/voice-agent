# 10. Accounts, privacy and call information  [audience: customer]

Calls with the assistant are handled by AI and may be passed to a human team member.

Call transcripts are kept for 30 days to resolve the request and improve the service, then deleted.

We only ask for details needed to find the order: order ID, first name, last 4 digits of the phone number, and delivery city.

This website is a demonstration project; no real orders or payments are processed.
