# 5. Payments  [audience: customer]

Accepted: debit/credit card (Visa, Mastercard, Verve), bank transfer, and pay on delivery.

Pay on delivery: Lagos only, orders up to ₦100,000, cash or card on the courier's POS.

Bank transfer orders are held for 24 hours; unpaid orders are cancelled automatically.

Charged twice: duplicate card charges are usually reversed by the bank automatically within 3 working days. If not, Shopdarret refunds the duplicate after checking (a human agent handles this).

Shopdarret never asks for card PINs, OTPs or full card numbers on a call.
