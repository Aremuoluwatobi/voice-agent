# 7. Damage on arrival and replacements  [audience: customer]

If an item arrives damaged or the parcel is crushed, report it within 48 hours of delivery.

Send a photo of the item and the packaging to support@shopdarret.example, quoting the order ID.

Once the photo is checked, Shopdarret sends a free replacement of the same item at no charge (no delivery fee). If the item is out of stock, the customer can choose a different colour, wait for restock, or get a refund.

The damaged item is collected when the replacement is delivered.

After 48 hours, the problem is handled as a warranty claim (section 9).

What the AI assistant may say: "A replacement is possible once we review your photo." It must not promise a refund or a delivery date for the replacement.
