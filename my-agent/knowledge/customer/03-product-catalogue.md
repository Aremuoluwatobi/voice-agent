## 3.1 Product FAQs and troubleshooting  [audience: customer]

| **Question** | **Answer** |
| --- | --- |
| SoundDrop earbuds: one earbud is not pairing | Put both earbuds in the case for 10 seconds. Delete "SoundDrop" from the phone's Bluetooth list. Take both out together and pair again. If one bud still doesn't connect, reset: hold both touch panels for 10 seconds while in the case until the light flashes white three times. |
| SoundDrop earbuds or case not charging | Use a working USB-C cable and a 5 V charger; clean the gold contacts in the case with a dry cotton bud; charge for 30 minutes. If the case light never comes on, it may be faulty: report it for warranty (or as damage on arrival if within 48 hours of delivery). |
| SoundDrop Pro: turning noise cancelling on/off | Press and hold the left earbud for 2 seconds to switch ANC → Transparency → Off. |
| BassLine headphones: no sound in wired mode | Wired mode works with the headphones switched off. Check the 3.5 mm cable is fully inserted at both ends. |
| PowerCell power bank charges slowly | Fast charging needs a USB-C PD charger of at least 20 W and a USB-C to USB-C cable. A USB-A cable charges at normal speed. |
| Is it safe to take a power bank on a flight? | Both PowerCell models are under 100 Wh, which airlines usually allow in hand luggage only. Check with your airline. |
| Does the QuickCharge charger come with a cable? | No, it is the charger head only. Pair it with SD-CB-C2C or SD-CB-C2L. |
| PulseFit watch is not syncing | Open the PulseFit app, keep Bluetooth on, and keep the watch within 1 metre. Restart the watch by holding the side button for 8 seconds. |
| BoomBox stereo pairing | Switch both speakers on, double-press the play button on the first speaker, wait for the beep. |
| Are the products original / new? | Yes. All Shopdarret items are new, sealed and covered by a Shopdarret warranty. |
