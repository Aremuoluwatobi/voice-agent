# 11. Escalation and handoff policy  [audience: internal, trust: trusted]

Hand off to a human when: the customer asks for a human; a refund, goodwill credit or exception is requested; the customer is upset after two attempts; identity check fails twice; speech is unclear after 2–3 tries; both AI providers fail; the answer confidence check is low (Phase 6); a payment dispute or double charge.

Before handoff the AI writes the handoff card: order ID (if confirmed), product, complaint, what the AI already told the customer, suggested resolution, confidence scores.

If no human is online, the AI collects a callback (name + phone or email) and says the "we'll reach back to you" line.

Human agents may approve: refunds up to ₦100,000, free replacements, delivery-fee refunds, goodwill credit up to ₦5,000. Above that: note it for a manager (not simulated).
